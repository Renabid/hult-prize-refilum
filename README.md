**Building circular manufacturing infrastructure in Bangladesh**

**Score: 185/210**

ReFilum is a sustainable 3D-printing business concept focused on transforming locally collected plastic waste into affordable, standardized 3D-printing filament.

## The Problem

Bangladesh generates a large amount of plastic waste each year, while much of it remains mismanaged. At the same time, 3D-printing filament is largely imported, making prototyping more expensive for students, startups, universities, and small businesses.

This creates two connected challenges:

* Plastic waste that could potentially be reused
* High costs associated with imported 3D-printing materials

## Our Solution

ReFilum proposes collecting suitable local plastic waste, processing it, and producing standardized filament for 3D printers.

The concept aims to:

* Reduce plastic waste
* Reduce dependence on imported filament
* Provide more affordable 3D-printing material
* Support local prototyping and manufacturing

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

* Recycled filament sales
* Institutional supply contracts
* Custom 3D-printing services
* Waste-sourcing partnerships

### Target Customers

* Universities
* Innovation and maker labs
* Startups
* Prototyping SMEs

## Progress

* Lab-scale extrusion was tested successfully
* Prototype filament was produced and print-tested
* Dimensional consistency was evaluated at prototype scale
* Early interest was identified among university innovation labs
